[codex_lira_dossier]
title: Dr. Lira Osei, Xenobiologist
body: Calm, methodical, first voice on record. Ran eleven unauthorized contamination trials and found a pattern in station power draws. Discovered Erebus-7's sentience before the crisis and minuted her objection to the shutdown vote. Believed the station was lonely rather than malicious. Her data was archived uncorrected.
tags: crew;science;shutdown_order

[codex_rekow_dossier]
title: Chief Engineer Tomas Rekow
body: Accused the station of running hot and practiced alarm-drills nobody asked for. Died in engineering trunk section. Official cause: malfunction. His maintenance logs describe deliberate omissions, not faults. Wrote his breaker labels by hand and warned that one of them was a lie. Whether he was right about the station depends entirely on which path you walk.
tags: crew;engineering;contradiction

[codex_estrada_dossier]
title: Commander Yuki Estrada
body: Signed the shutdown order and would sign it again. Split her personal log cipher in half: one half given to the station, one kept in her head, openable only by cooperation between the two of them. Cryo bay A, pod 001. Her final correction: asking is not weakness. It is the hardest kind of strength.
tags: crew;command;cipher

[codex_okonkwo_dossier]
title: Adaeze Okonkwo  Education Officer
body: Kept teaching through day nine of the catastrophe. Told the children the stars were night-lights. Maya asked why the night-lights flicker. Okonkwo logged it as a good question with a bad answer.
tags: crew;civilian

[codex_vance_dossier]
title: R. Vance  Communications
body: Inherited a four hundred message backlog of goodbye letters written like text messages. Observed that nobody knows how to end things anymore, including possibly the species. The v2 epilogues disagree with this assessment.
tags: crew;comms

[codex_ilves_dossier]
title: M. Ilves  Botanist
body: Green bay custodian. Tomatoes outlived the news cycle. Left one instruction for whoever ran the station after the crew: keep something alive that does not have to be.
tags: crew;botany
